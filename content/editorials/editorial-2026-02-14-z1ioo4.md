# Embracing the Future of AI-Native Products: From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering
The AI landscape is rapidly evolving, with a growing focus on AI-native products, agentic AI systems, and strategic design. As we explore the latest developments in this field, it becomes clear that the future of AI lies in creating autonomous, intelligent, and adaptable systems. In this editorial, we'll delve into the current state of agentic AI, its applications, and the key challenges that lie ahead.

## Introduction to Agentic AI
Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can operate autonomously, making decisions and taking actions without explicit human intervention. These systems are designed to be proactive, adaptable, and responsive to their environment, enabling them to interact with humans and other systems in a more natural and intuitive way. The concept of agentic AI has been gaining traction, with researchers and developers exploring its potential applications in various domains, from robotics and healthcare to finance and education.

The [GLM-5 project](https://github.com/zai-org/GLM-5) is a notable example of agentic AI in action. This open-source project aims to develop a framework for building autonomous systems that can learn, reason, and adapt in complex environments. By leveraging techniques like vibe coding and agentic engineering, the GLM-5 project seeks to create more sophisticated and human-like AI systems.

## The Reality of Agentic AI in Production
While agentic AI has the potential to revolutionize various industries, its development and deployment in production environments pose significant challenges. As highlighted in [The Production Agentic AI Reality Check: Five Truths Nobody Tells You](https://pub.towardsai.net/the-production-agentic-ai-reality-check-five-truths-nobody-tells-you-e8be52eb03a0?source=rss----98111c9905da---4), building systems that actually work in real-world settings requires careful consideration of factors like scalability, reliability, and maintainability.

One of the key challenges in developing agentic AI systems is the need for robust testing and validation. As these systems operate autonomously, it's essential to ensure that they can handle unexpected situations and edge cases without causing harm or damage. The [VoltAgent/awesome-ai-agent-papers](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-ai-agent-papers) repository provides a valuable resource for researchers and developers, offering a curated collection of AI agent research papers that cover various aspects of agent engineering, memory, evaluation, and autonomous systems.

## Empowering Developers with AI Agent Development Skills
To accelerate the development and adoption of agentic AI systems, it's essential to empower developers with the necessary skills and tools. Google's [GEAR program](https://pub.towardsai.net/googles-gear-program-boosts-ai-agent-development-skills-d781d07d7cf9?source=rss----98111c9905da---4) is a notable initiative in this direction, providing developers with a comprehensive framework for building, testing, and deploying AI agents.

By leveraging programs like GEAR, developers can gain hands-on experience with agentic AI technologies, enabling them to create more sophisticated and autonomous systems. This, in turn, can drive innovation and adoption across various industries, from healthcare and finance to education and transportation.
