## Strategic Design Considerations
As we design AI-native products, it's essential to consider the strategic implications of agentic AI. The development of [Claude-context-os](https://github.com/Arkya-AI/claude-context-os) by [Arkya-AI](https://github.com/Arkya-AI/claude-context-os) is a notable example of how context-aware systems can prevent context loss and enable reliable multi-session work. This approach has significant implications for product design, as it highlights the importance of context-aware systems that can adapt to changing user needs.

When designing AI-native products, we must prioritize strategic design considerations, such as:

* **Context-aware systems**: Designing systems that can understand and adapt to changing contexts is crucial for building effective AI-native products.
* **Agentic autonomy**: Empowering AI systems to make decisions and take actions autonomously is essential for creating agentic AI products that can operate effectively in real-world scenarios.
* **Human-AI collaboration**: Designing products that facilitate seamless collaboration between humans and AI systems is critical for maximizing the potential of agentic AI.
